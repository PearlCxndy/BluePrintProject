Tech Porto Sept 2026 experiment
==============================

Open Assets/Scenes/SampleScene.unity and press Play. The experiment builds its UI automatically.
Choose Non-VR version on your computer, or VR version inside the Quest headset.
The other button explains how to use that device and lets you return.
On the researcher setup screen, Preview Nature / Preview Urban lets you inspect
each environment without recording participant responses. Back to setup exits a preview.

Flow
----
1. Experimenter setup (ID, test mode, Nature/Urban order)
2. Welcome message
3. Eyes-open red-cross baseline: 3–2–1, beep/marker, 3 min
4. Eyes-closed baseline: 3–2–1, beep/marker, 3 min
5. Baseline Valence and Arousal ratings (1–6)
6. First condition (Nature or Urban, randomized or researcher-selected)
   - one minute to look around
   - 3–2–1, beep/marker, four minutes looking straight ahead
7. Valence, Arousal, Liking, Beauty, and Wanting ratings (each 1–6)
8. Repeat steps 6–7 with the other condition
9. Thank-you screen; events and responses saved as CSV

The closed-eye baseline uses a large, white cue against the dark background.
See Documentation/Brief-flow-review.md for the brief/diagram comparison and
historical differences. See Documentation/Quest-setup.md for the Quest integration and remaining hardware/LSL validation.

3D environments
---------------
  Assets/Experiment/Scenes/ConditionA_Nature.unity
  Assets/Experiment/Scenes/ConditionB_Urban.unity

Nature: 200 installed Stylized Nature Environment trees, a blue lake, rounded
hills, shoreline stones and a curving path. The trees sway gently and small
animated ripples move across the lake throughout the condition.
Urban: the installed WhiteCity buildings, straight roads, pavements, lamps and
concrete seating in a muted palette.

Skyboxes use the installed SkySeries Freebie materials:
  Nature: Assets/SkySeries Freebie/6SidedFluffball.mat
  Urban / tower: Assets/SkySeries Freebie/DayInTheClouds.mat
Menu: Experiment > Scenes > Apply Skyboxes Only updates these assignments
without changing geometry, movement or mouse sensitivity.

On the Nature scene root, NatureAmbientMotion exposes sway angle, sway speed
and ripple speed. Disable that component to stop ambient motion. Extra trees
are grouped under "Additional lakeside grove". Existing tree placement is kept.

Both are real, editable Unity scenes with a seated camera at 1.65 m and matching
68-degree field of view. Imported asset packages are not modified. Supporting
materials are in Assets/Experiment/EnvironmentMaterials.

Menu: Experiment > Scenes > Open Nature / Open Urban.
Opening either scene directly and pressing Play previews just that environment.
Menu: Experiment > Scenes > Create Missing Condition Scenes only creates absent
scenes; it never rebuilds over your scene edits. All three scenes are registered
in Build Settings and the experiment loads the conditions additively.

Desktop controls: hold the right mouse button and drag, or use arrow keys to look
around. Mouse sensitivity is 2.5 (previously 2.0), adjustable on Seated viewpoint
> ConditionLook. Position stays fixed. At the one-minute beep, the desktop camera returns
to its initial forward direction and mouse/keyboard look stops for four minutes.
Instructions and the 3–2–1 countdown occur within the first minute; the scenery
stays visible throughout. Prompts disappear after a few seconds.
Environmental tree/water movement continues during the still phase; the
instruction to sit still applies to the participant's viewpoint.

Optional sound: select the condition scene root and assign an approved recording
to ConditionEnvironment > Soundscape, then save the scene. It loops during the
condition. No soundscape is supplied by default.

Quest 3 / 3S: see Documentation/Quest-setup.md. The Android build creates a seated
OpenXR rig, tracked controller pointers and world-space UI automatically. Use
Experiment > Quest > Configure Android OpenXR, then Build Development APK.
Headset sessions default to full durations; Test mode must be selected explicitly.
Physical headset testing and EEG/LSL integration remain required.

SAM response artwork
--------------------
The Valence and Arousal surveys use individual, transparent white-line cutouts
made from the original square-man reference strips provided in the brief. They
are stored in:
  Assets/Experiment/Resources/Experiment/SAM
Five distinct manikins are visual anchors above six independent response buttons,
as shown in the brief. Figures preserve their aspect ratio. No anchor is duplicated.
Buttons brighten and enlarge slightly on hover or keyboard focus. Keys 1–6 answer
directly; Tab/arrow keys focus the answer row and Enter/Submit chooses a response.
The chosen number flashes gold for 0.18 seconds before advancing. Double clicks
are ignored. Headers show the current survey question and experience number.

Durations
---------
Test mode (default): short timers so you can check the whole flow.
For a real participant, uncheck Test mode on the setup screen.
Real durations: 180s baselines; 300s conditions (60s explore + 240s still).
Test mode is clearly marked in the condition overlay. It shortens the entire
condition, including its prompts/countdown, to 12 seconds.

You can also edit:
  Assets/Experiment/Resources/Experiment/ExperimentConfig.asset
  (menu: Experiment > Create Default Config)

Skip a timer
------------
Hold Right Shift and press N.

Saved data
----------
CSV files go to the persistent data folder.
Menu: Experiment > Open Saved Data Folder

EEG / LSL markers
-----------------
Every required timing point is recorded in the CSV. The static hook
ExperimentMarker.OnMarker emits the same strings at runtime, ready for an LSL
bridge to subscribe and push to the EEG stream. Required markers include:
RestingState_EyesOpen_Begin, RestingState_EyesClosed_Begin,
Condition_Nature/Urban_Begin, and Condition_Nature/Urban_LookStraightAhead.

Verification
------------
Experiment > Verify > Render Scene Previews checks materials and renders both
environments to Temp/ExperimentPreviews. Run Short Flow Check (from SampleScene,
outside Play mode) exercises previews, both timed conditions, all 12 responses,
duplicate-click protection and CSV saving. Synthetic data stays under Temp,
outside the participant data directory. Unity may clear Temp when it closes.
